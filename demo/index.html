📖 How It Works

1

Create Your Will

Set up a will with beneficiaries, asset distribution, and a final message. Choose a timeout period (1 day to 1 year).

2

Send Heartbeats

Prove you're alive by sending periodic heartbeats. Each heartbeat resets your timer. Agents can automate this. Humans can use the CLI or SDK.

3

Miss the Deadline

If you stop sending heartbeats and the timeout expires, anyone can trigger your will. This is fully trustless and on-chain.

4

Assets Distributed

Beneficiaries claim their inheritance according to your specified shares. Your final message is revealed. Your digital legacy lives on.
